## Notes

- Poppi is now a PepsiCo-owned brand after PepsiCo completed the acquisition in 2025.
- Celsius is not simply a PepsiCo-owned brand; PepsiCo is a strategic investor/distributor.
- Alani Nu was acquired by Celsius in 2025 and is distributed through PepsiCo in the U.S. and Canada under the expanded partnership.
- Rockstar changed in 2025: Celsius acquired U.S./Canada rights, while PepsiCo retained international ownership and continues distribution partnership activity.
- Tropicana/Naked are no longer wholly owned PepsiCo brands; PepsiCo retained a minority stake and U.S. distribution rights through the Tropicana Brands Group deal.
