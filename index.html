How to work this store

  1. Start front: fill checkout coolers and queue singles before any aisle work.
  2. Clear front displays: lobby, seasonal, and ad stacks while you are already up front.
  3. Check deli: find rotisserie chicken and service the mini-can chicken-rack display if present.
  4. Work B|4 by section: sections 2 and 4 first, then 6, 8, 12, and 14.
  5. Do heavy pack work first: 24 packs/cubes, 12 packs, mini cans, 2 liters, then small formats.
  6. Finish back: overstock, credits, cardboard, and route-owner notes near receiving.
